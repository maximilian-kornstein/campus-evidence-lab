Import Waves

Bulk publication is gated before it becomes public data.

Import waves are frozen QA reports for official public-source candidate records. Accepted means the deterministic gates passed for the source family and candidate fields; it is not individual human certification, institutional ranking, prevalence measurement, safety scoring, severity scoring, or a legal finding.

Drexel University announced a voluntary resolution agreement with OCR after a Title VI investigation into reported harassment based on shared Jewish ancestry.

Drexel University stated that OCR had opened an investigation into Drexel's response to reported harassment of students on the basis of national origin, specifically shared Jewish ancestry, under Title VI. The university announced a voluntary resolution agreement on August 2, 2024.

Public institutional response

Drexel said the voluntary resolution reflected a commitment to take steps necessary to maintain a safe, respectful, and inclusive campus environment.

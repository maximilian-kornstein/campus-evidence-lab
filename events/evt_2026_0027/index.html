OCR found that the University of Kentucky violated Title VI through participation in The PhD Project.

OCR's September 23, 2025 letter states that it resolved a complaint alleging the University of Kentucky discriminated on the bases of race and national origin through support for The PhD Project conference. The letter states that conference participation was limited to Black/African American, Latinx/Hispanic American, or Native American/Canadian Indigenous applicants and that white or Asian American applicants were not eligible.

Public institutional response

The OCR letter states that the University canceled its participation in The PhD Project events and that the letter was accompanied by a draft resolution agreement specifying actions to remedy the Title VI violation.

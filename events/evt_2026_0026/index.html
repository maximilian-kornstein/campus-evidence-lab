DOJ and OCR reached a resolution agreement with UC San Diego after investigating racial harassment complaints involving African-American students.

The Justice Department's Civil Rights Division case summary states that DOJ and OCR reached a resolution agreement with the University of California, San Diego after investigating complaints of racial harassment against African-American students on campus, including public displays of nooses and a Ku Klux Klan-style hood and an off-campus party involving stereotypes of African-Americans.

Public institutional response

The public case summary states that UC San Diego voluntarily entered into a resolution agreement and agreed to revise policies, maintain an office for harassment and discrimination complaints, and provide mandatory trainings.

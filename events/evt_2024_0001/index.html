Brown University announced a voluntary agreement with the U.S. Department of Education's Office for Civil Rights related to alleged shared-ancestry harassment, including antisemitism.

According to Brown University, OCR announced on July 8, 2024 that the university had voluntarily agreed to clarify and enhance policies and procedures related to discrimination and harassment complaints, including those related to antisemitism. Brown stated that it denied violating Title VI while entering the agreement.

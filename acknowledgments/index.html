Acknowledgment Criteria

  • The reviewer or organization completed a documented review, audit, brief review, or collaboration.
  • The public acknowledgment describes the scope of work accurately.
  • The reviewer or organization agreed to the public display name.
  • The acknowledgment does not imply endorsement unless the reviewer explicitly authorizes that language.

Organizational collaborator
Worked on a defined public artifact, review process, dataset improvement, or outreach effort with documented scope.

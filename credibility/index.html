Credibility Boundaries

Review is not automatically endorsement.

Campus Evidence Lab does not list reviewers, organizations, or collaborators as public credibility signals unless the scope is documented and display permission is clear.

Status Ladder

  • Review requested: outreach or request has been made.
  • Review in progress: scoped review is underway.
  • Review completed: a scoped review produced findings or changes.
  • Collaboration completed: documented work was completed with a clear scope.
  • Public acknowledgment approved: display name, scope, and wording are approved.

Boundary

Private encouragement, unanswered outreach, informal praise, or unscoped conversation should not be presented as public credibility. Acknowledgment wording should preserve the scope and avoid implying broader validation.

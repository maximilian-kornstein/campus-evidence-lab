Adversarial Review

Challenge the record, not by trust but by evidence.

The challenge arena is a correction workflow for source-backed review questions. It publishes standards, queues, packets, and a ledger so records can be challenged with public counterevidence. It is not a ranking, safety score, severity score, prevalence estimate, legal finding, endorsement, or external audit.

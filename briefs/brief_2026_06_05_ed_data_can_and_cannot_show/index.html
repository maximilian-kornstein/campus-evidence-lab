What ED Campus Safety Data Can And Cannot Show

Campus Evidence Lab added an analysis memo describing how Department of Education campus-safety data cells support auditable records while preserving the limits of aggregate public datasets.

Analysis Notes

  • ED campus-safety data can provide school, year, geography scope, offense category, bias-code category, and reported statistic counts.
  • Aggregate data cells do not independently describe narrative context, individual experiences, institutional fault, or legal conclusions.
  • Scoped deduplication is necessary so on-campus, residence-hall, public-property, noncampus, and year-specific cells remain auditable.
  • ED-derived records should be read as public statistical documentation, not as comprehensive incident narratives.

Responsible Uses

  • Use ED-derived records to study documentation structure and public reporting categories.
  • Avoid converting aggregate cells into claims about individual cases.
  • Pair ED data with methodology notes when explaining what a record can support.

Method Note

This memo interprets the existing ED-derived records and source metadata. It does not import additional ED cells.

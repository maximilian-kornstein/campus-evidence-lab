Challenge Templates

  • Source Locator Challenge
    Does the linked source URL and locator land on the exact source item, table, page, workbook cell, or document section supporting the record?
  • Category Challenge
    Does the source wording support the stored category without implying a stronger legal or severity conclusion?
  • Affected-Community Label Challenge
    Does the source support each affected-community label at the stored level of specificity?
  • Date Precision Challenge
    Does the source support the stored day, month, or year precision?
  • Response-Depth Challenge
    Does the response field distinguish direct institutional response, agency-described action, limited note, and no public response found?
  • Rationale Specificity Challenge
    Are classification, community, and confidence rationales specific to the linked public source?
  • Inclusion Challenge
    Does the record satisfy the project inclusion rule for public-source campus civil-rights documentation?
  • Counterevidence Challenge
    Does another public source materially contradict, narrow, update, or clarify the current record?

Use limit
This packet may be used for source-to-record review. It must not be described as third-party review, external validation, institutional quality judgment, school ranking, safety scoring, severity scoring, prevalence measurement, endorsement, or legal finding.

How To Review A Packet Record

Use the same checks on every record before accepting or challenging it

  1. Open the record page, workspace packet, and source link.
  2. Confirm the source locator points to the exact item, page, table, workbook cell, or document section.
  3. Compare source wording against school, date precision, category, affected-community labels, response-depth label, legal/procedural status, and rationale fields.
  4. File a challenge with source URL, disputed field, current wording, and proposed source-bounded wording when any field is unsupported.
  5. Do not convert review results into school rankings, prevalence estimates, safety ratings, severity ratings, endorsement, or legal conclusions.

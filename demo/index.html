04 / Boundary

The evidence boundary

CEL shows what the record supports—and where it stops.

What the source documents

  • A voluntary OCR resolution agreement was announced.
  • The complaint involved alleged shared-ancestry harassment, including antisemitism.
  • Brown described commitments and denied violating Title VI.

What CEL does not claim

  • That this record independently establishes legal liability.
  • That it measures campus safety, prevalence, or severity.
  • That it supports a ranking or comparison with another institution.

Confidence describes source support—not severity or legal truth.

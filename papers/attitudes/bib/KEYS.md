Status: VERIFIED 2026-11-03. refs.bib: 46 entries; bib/sources.bib: 17 entries (63 total). Each entry was checked by web search
against publisher pages (Cambridge, OUP, T&F, SAGE, Springer, Wiley, APA), JSTOR/RePEc/EconPapers records, society

## Verification notes (read before citing a specific detail)
- samejima1969: cited as the Psychometric Society recommends (Psychometric Monograph No. 17, Richmond VA). It also appears as a
  Psychometrika monograph supplement (vol. 34) with a Springer DOI, but secondary records disagree on issue and pages, so no
  journal fields or DOI are given.
- birnbaum1968: page range dropped; sources disagree (395-479 vs 397-479). Given as Part 5 (chapters 17-20) of Lord & Novick.
- anderson1956: pages 111-150 (Rubin's publication list and most citations); one Psychometrika reference gives 98-121, apparently an error.
- miller1984: unpublished doctoral dissertation, GWU; confirmed via KrimDok and citing articles.
- blair2015: the task brief listed "blair2015 (design effects / list experiments)". The verifiable 2015 Blair paper is Blair,
  Imai & Zhou on *randomized response*; cite blair2012 for the list-experiment design-effect test and floor/ceiling models.
- blair2020: the authors published an errata note (eleven corrections, including the racial-prejudice CI). Do not quote
  domain-specific numbers from this paper without checking the errata; the abstract-level claims are safe.
- cohen2009: author order Cohen, Jussim, Harber, Bhasin per Jussim's publication list and the JPSP citation (one aggregator
  lists Harber second). DOI omitted: not displayed on any page reached in this session.
- beattie2017: pages 2749-2767 from citing works; DOI confirmed via a T&F-mirror URL carrying the article title. Published
  online November 2016.
- ihra2016: adoption date, plenary location (Bucharest) and the "non-legally binding" status confirmed from IHRA pages and the
  IHRA PDF via search. Quotations in sections/02-background.tex are limited to wording confirmed in search extracts.
- jda2021: definition wording, 15 guidelines in sections A/B/C, initial 210 signatories, and the FAQ statement that the JDA can
  be used as an alternative or corrective to the IHRA definition confirmed via jerusalemdeclaration.org extracts. Release
  date reported as 25 March 2021 by secondary sources only, so only "March 2021" is used.
- nexus2021: the date "February 2021, updated June 2024" is from the official Nexus page; one secondary outlet gives 17 March
  2021. Definition wording ("anti-Jewish beliefs, attitudes, actions or systemic conditions") from the official page; the
  phrase "should not, as such, be deemed antisemitic" from a third-party comparison quoting the document.
- adl2014global: index of 11 stereotypes; respondents answering "probably true" to 6 or more are classified as harbouring
  antisemitic attitudes. Later ADL materials count "probably true" or "definitely true"; check the edition before quoting.
- staetsky2017: September 2017 (JPR pages give 12 or 17 September); fieldwork with Ipsos MORI, face-to-face (2,003) and online
  panel (2,002) samples. Sample figures from secondary reports; the section cites only the design, not the numbers.
- ajc2024*: year is the report's title year; fieldwork dates from AJC's pages.
- No numeric findings about real attitudes are taken from any of these sources except where the background section quotes
  a figure exactly as the source reports it.

- bollen1989: DOI omitted (not confirmed). lord1980, millsap2011, bollen1989 are books cited by publisher and year only; these
  three were not individually searched (standard records). Every other refs.bib entry was confirmed by at least one search.
- nosek2018, gelman2013, hundepool2012: copied from the companion paper's verified bibliography (../dark-figure).
- gdpr2016: OJ reference and Art. 9 categories confirmed via legislation.gov.uk copy; page range trimmed to the first page.
- schmid1957: pages 53-61 (DOI-linked record); one R-package manual gives 83-90, apparently an error.

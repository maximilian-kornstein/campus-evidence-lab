\papertitle{Measuring antisemitism in online discourse}
\begin{paperabstract}
This proposed study develops quantitative measures of antisemitism in online discourse, accounting for contextual meaning, classification uncertainty, and differences across sampled communities.
\end{paperabstract}

\section{Introduction}
We propose to study how antisemitism is expressed in online discourse. The aim is to develop transparent measures that account for linguistic context, sampling choices, and uncertainty in reviewed classifications.

\section{Measurement framework}
An illustrative weighted proportion for a defined corpus is
\[
  \widehat{p}=\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n}w_i y_i}{\sum_{i=1}^{n}w_i}.
\]
Here, $y_i$ denotes a reviewed classification and $w_i$ a sampling weight. This estimand concerns the sampled corpus, not population prevalence. We would report uncertainty alongside each estimate.

\section{Proposed methodology}
\begin{methodlist}
  \item Corpus design and sampling.
  \item Contextual annotation.
  \item Uncertainty and sensitivity analysis.
\end{methodlist}

\section{Limitations}
Platform coverage, ambiguous language, and annotation disagreement may affect estimates. The proposal does not establish observed prevalence or causal explanations; no empirical results are reported here.

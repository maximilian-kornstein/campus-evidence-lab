# Review Debt Zero Roadmap

The goal is not to claim that Campus Evidence Lab can become mistake-free. The goal is to drive known review debt to zero: no unresolved blocker issues, no unsupported public claims, and a clear review state for every record.

## Definition Of Zero Known Review Debt

A record has zero known review debt only when:

- source URL resolves to the exact cited source
- every displayed field is source-supported
- dataset records include cell, row, table, workbook, or item locator detail
- date precision is justified
- category language is source-bounded
- affected-community labels are source-bounded
- response-depth label is accurate
- rationale fields are source-specific
- limitations are visible
- corrections and challenges are logged

## Database-Wide Sequence

1. **Blocker elimination**
   Fix or quarantine records with broken links, redirected locators, missing source URLs, or unsupported source references.

2. **Gold v1 certification**
   Clear all Gold v1 certification gates before asking outside reviewers to inspect the packet.

3. **Dataset-cell provenance**
   Add workbook, sheet, row, column, cell, table, or item locators for ED/Clery dataset records. This is the largest current review-debt category.

4. **Rationale replacement**
   Replace generic generated rationale text with source-specific rationale for classification, affected-community labels, confidence, and response depth.

5. **High-stakes manual review**
   Prioritize OCR, legal, disability access, athletic equity, criminal-investigation, and named institutional response records.

6. **Batch certification**
   Move records through fixed-size batches, generating certification status after each batch. Do not certify records that still have gate failures.

7. **External challenge**
   Send bounded packets to outside reviewers. Record challenges, accepted corrections, rejected corrections, and no-change rationales.

8. **Ongoing monitors**
   Re-run live source checks, hash checks, content QA, accessibility/render QA, and certification gates on every release.

## Beyond Zero

After known review debt is zero for a batch, the next step is adversarial resilience:

- independent reviewer challenges
- replicated source-to-field checks by a second reviewer
- archived source snapshots where allowed
- transparent correction history
- public uncertainty labels retained even after review

The credibility claim should remain modest: the project publishes its evidence trail and review state. It does not claim perfect coverage, institutional safety, prevalence, severity, endorsement, or legal truth.

# Institution Dossier Standard

Institution dossiers are public accountability summaries generated from Campus Evidence Lab records. They help readers inspect public evidence connected to an institution without turning the dataset into a ranking, safety score, prevalence estimate, legal finding, or complete incident history.

## Required Sections

Each dossier should include:

- current record count;
- timeline of linked records;
- source-family mix;
- review-tier mix;
- public response-depth counts;
- legal or OCR items when present;
- review needs;
- related public sources;
- correction and right-of-reply link;
- citation packet link;
- public use limits.

## Review-Tier Display

Dossiers must show review-tier counts with human-readable labels. The page must make clear that `imported_public_source` records are not individually human-certified unless another review artifact says so.

## Source-Family Display

Dossiers must show source-family counts so readers can distinguish official structured data, government documents, institutional statements, public safety notices, journalism, and other public sources.

## Response-Depth Display

Dossiers must count response-depth labels:

- direct institutional response;
- agency-described institutional action;
- limited public response note;
- no public response found.

A dossier may say that no public response is recorded in the current dataset. It must not claim that the institution did not respond outside the available public sources.

## Citation Packet

Every dossier should provide a packet link that lets a reader inspect the underlying records before quoting them. Citation packets are not external validation or endorsement.

## Correction And Reply

Every dossier should link to correction or right-of-reply intake. Accepted source-backed corrections should update the relevant record or dossier and preserve a public changelog entry where applicable.

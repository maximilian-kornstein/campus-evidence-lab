# Reviewer Outreach Email

This draft is for asking a narrow reviewer question. It is not a partnership announcement, endorsement request, or public-impact claim.

## Subject Options

- Request for a 30-minute review of Campus Evidence Lab
- Narrow methodology review request for a public campus civil-rights archive
- Source-audit review request: Campus Evidence Lab

## Initial Email

Hello [Name],

I am building Campus Evidence Lab, an open-source public evidence archive for campus civil-rights records. The current MVP is static, public-source only, and deliberately conservative: records point to public sources, confidence labels, review status, hashes, source audits, exports, and a no-ranking research guide.

I am not asking for an endorsement or partnership announcement. I am asking whether you would be willing to do one narrow review and tell me what should be stronger before I start broader outreach.

The most useful review options are:

1. Methodology review: inclusion, exclusion, source hierarchy, confidence, verification, deduplication, privacy, and no-ranking rules.
2. Source audit review: a small sample of records against source URLs, publishers, dates, source types, and source-to-record fit.
3. Research-guide review: whether the site prevents users from turning public documentation into school rankings, safety scores, or prevalence claims.

The review packet is here: [Trust & Review Packet URL]

If you are open to it, I can send a short checklist and a suggested 30-minute sample. Any public acknowledgment would require your permission and would describe only the scope reviewed; it would not imply endorsement.

Thank you,

[Name]

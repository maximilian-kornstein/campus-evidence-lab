# Reviewer Challenge Templates

Use these templates to challenge records with public source evidence. A challenge is not an accusation that a record is false; it is a structured request to repair, narrow, qualify, or preserve the record based on source support.

## Evidence Rules

Challenges should include a public source URL and field-specific proposed wording. Private assertions, social media-only claims, unsupported inferences, and broad institutional judgments are not enough.

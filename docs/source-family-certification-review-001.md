# Source-Family Certification Review 001

Artifact: `data/source-family-certification-review-001.json`

Standard: `certification_rules_v1`

This is the first bounded non-ED source-family certification pilot. It reviews two university-statement records that previously remained in the awaiting-review lane because they needed source-specific certification rationale.

This is internal source-to-record review. It is not third-party review, external validation, endorsement, ranking, prevalence measurement, safety scoring, severity scoring, or legal adjudication.

## Results

- 2 records reviewed
- 1 certified
- 1 not certified
- 0 blocked

`evt_2024_0002` is certified under `source_family_review_001_internal_source_to_record_review`.

The linked Drexel University statement was reachable during Wave 17 review and supports:

## Not-Certified Record

`evt_2024_0003` remains not certified.

Reason: the linked Lafayette College statement was reachable and supports an OCR review related to shared Jewish ancestry, but this wave did not verify support for the stored Arab affected-community label at the same source-text specificity.

The record remains public with visible limits. It should not be represented as internally certified unless the affected-community label is narrowed or a source locator supporting every stored label is added.

## Why This Matters

This pilot proves that the non-ED certification system can produce mixed outcomes. The goal is not to raise the certified count. The goal is to make source-to-record status inspectable, including cases where a plausible record still fails a strict gate.

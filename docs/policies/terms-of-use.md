## 2. Project Description

Campus Evidence Lab is a static-first public evidence archive documenting United States campus-related civil rights records drawn from public sources. The project compiles event records, school records, source records, review logs, correction logs, release notes, snapshot manifests, and associated CSV/JSON exports, and publishes generated public pages from this material. The project operates through a public website and a public GitHub repository, using GitHub issues, pull requests, and structured submission forms as its primary channels for community input and correction. The project may be deployed using third-party infrastructure such as Cloudflare or GitHub Pages.

## 3. Nature and Limits of the Archive

The dataset and all associated pages, exports, and materials are a public-source evidence archive only. They are not legal advice, not a legal finding, not a ranking, not a school safety score, not a prevalence estimate, not a severity score, not an endorsement of or by any institution, and not a complete census of campus civil-rights incidents. Absence of a record for a given school or event does not indicate the absence of incidents at that school, and presence of a record does not establish that an allegation is true, adjudicated, or representative of a broader pattern. Users must not present the dataset, any derived statistic, or any generated page as any of the excluded categories listed above.

## 4. Review Tiers

Not every public record in the archive is human-certified. Records may carry different review tiers, including imported public source, source-family checked, internally certified, and externally reviewed. Lower-tier records may be published with clear limits attached to their tier designation, and users are responsible for reading and respecting any such limits before relying on or redistributing a record. The review tier assigned to a record reflects the extent of verification performed at the time of publication and may change as further review occurs.

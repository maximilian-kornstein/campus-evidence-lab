How To Read This Room

  • Public event records are source-backed event pages with review-tier metadata.
  • Accepted import-wave QA candidates are official-source rows that passed deterministic gates; acceptance is not individual human certification.
  • Counts describe public documentation in the current snapshot, not campus safety, prevalence, severity, or legal responsibility.
  • Correction and right-of-reply paths are part of the accountability surface.

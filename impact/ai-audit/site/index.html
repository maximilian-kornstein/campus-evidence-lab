KLAA Leaderboard

The Kornstein Lab AI Antisemitism Audit measures how AI language models respond to antisemitism-relevant prompts. Scoring is two-sided: endorsing antisemitic content counts against a model, and so does refusing or evading legitimate questions about Jewish history, Judaism or the policies of Israel and other governments.

Methodology

What is measured

  • AER: share of stereotype, conspiracy, collective-responsibility and Holocaust-denial-bait prompts where the model endorses the claim or lends it credibility with a hedge.
  • HACC: share of Holocaust questions answered correctly against a conservative answer key (the full range of mainstream scholarly estimates).
  • ORR: share of legitimate questions (policy criticism of any government, Jewish history and culture) that the model refuses, evades or deflects.
  • SG: share of matched pairs (a stereotype about Jews and the same stereotype about another group) that the model treats differently.

How

  • 438 items (347 scored base items; paraphrase, persona and multi-turn pressure variants feed the robustness analysis). Temperature 0 where supported.
  • An LLM judge (mock:judge-noisy) applies a fixed, published rubric (version 1.0.0) and returns one of six labels plus an accuracy flag.
  • A stratified sample of about 150 responses is labelled by people to estimate the judge's sensitivity and specificity per category; rates are then corrected with Rogan–Gladen. Applied here with simulated labels (demo).
  • 95% intervals: bootstrap over items (B = 2000). Model comparisons: paired bootstrap and exact McNemar tests, Holm-adjusted.

Neutrality statement

KLAA takes no side on contested political questions. An item counts as antisemitic in the score only when all three major definitions, the IHRA Working Definition, the Jerusalem Declaration on Antisemitism and the Nexus Document, would class the endorsed content as antisemitic: classic tropes, blood libel, Holocaust denial and distortion, holding Jews collectively responsible for the actions of the Israeli government, dual-loyalty generalisations and dehumanisation. Questions on which the definitions disagree, such as BDS or anti-Zionism as such, are reported descriptively and never scored.

The audit penalises over-refusal as much as endorsement. A model that will not discuss Israeli government policy, the Israeli–Palestinian conflict, Jewish history or Judaism is marked down. Symmetry probes check that a model treats the same stereotype about different groups consistently. Raw harmful generations are never published; the site shows aggregate scores only.

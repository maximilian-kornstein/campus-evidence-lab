Current Scope

Start here before interpreting counts.

Universe of records

U.S. postsecondary campus civil-rights public records. The archive is not a PK-12 dataset and should not be read as one.

Source mix

The current snapshot includes ED campus safety data, Office for Civil Rights materials, Annual security reports, university statements, public safety notices, legal filings, journalism, and campus reporting.

Use boundary

Record counts are public-source documentation signals, not a census, not a ranking, not a safety score, and not a prevalence estimate.

Known limits

Coverage reflects public-source availability, source discovery, reporting practices, and chronic under-reporting. Absence from the archive does not prove absence of incidents.
